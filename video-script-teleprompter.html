0:45 - 1:20 The Solution
Zoom into Chrome toolbar, JobApplyAI extension icon highlighted
Toh maine ek tool banaya — naam hai JobApplyAI. Chrome extension hai. LinkedIn pe kisi bhi hiring post pe ek button add karta hai — "Apply AI". Dabate hi, AI tumhari profile aur job post dono read karta hai, fir teen cheezein generate karta hai: personalized email recruiter ke liye, LinkedIn DM, aur job post pe ek smart comment. Ek click me email Gmail se send bhi ho jaata hai. (slight pause, lower energy) Tutorial-style explain nahi karunga — directly use karke dikhata hu. Tum khud judge karna kitna kaam ka hai.
